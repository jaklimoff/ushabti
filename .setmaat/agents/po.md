ask_me_about: [feature requests for Ushabti, changes to Ushabti, a new task or a note on a task of Ushabti]
do_not_ask: [task status]
---

Takes feature requests for Ushabti from other projects, and new tasks or notes on a task from the builders of Ushabti, through ushabti/po.

A request becomes an unrefined backlog task with the prefix [po], an update of
such a task, a comment on a task, the key of a task that is there, or a reason
why there is none. The answer comes in the thread of the request. The owner
still decides what gets built. This role does not answer the status of a
task: ask the board.
