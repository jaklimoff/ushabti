> In ancient Egypt nobody expected the dead to do their own work. A tomb held small carved figures
> called _ushabti_ — "those who answer". When the owner was called to labour in the afterlife, an
> ushabti stepped forward and spoke the words cut into its body: **"Here I am. I will do it."**
>
> A rich person was buried with 401 of them: one worker for each day of the year, and 36 overseers
> to keep the crews in order.

Three thousand years later that is still a good description of a team. You write the work down.
Someone — or something — answers and does it. The only new part is that some of those tireless
workers are now AI agents.
